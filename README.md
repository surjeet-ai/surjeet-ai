### 🧠 What I Build

I turn ideas, repetitive workflows, business data, and complex processes into **practical AI-powered applications** — not just demos.

- 🤖 **AI Agents** — research, lead intelligence, content strategy, business analysis, customer support, productivity, data-processing
- 🧩 **AI Assistants** — internal knowledge, customer support, research, sales, content, document analysis
- ⚙️ **AI Automation** — `Data → AI Processing → Decision → Automation → Output`
- 🎥 **AI Content & Creator Systems** — content research, trend analysis, strategy, post/script generation, scheduling
- 📊 **AI & Data Applications** — lead scoring, document analysis, classification, extraction, BI dashboards

### 🛠️ My Approach

**Understand → Design → Build → Integrate → Test → Deploy → Improve**

I don't add AI because it's trending — I pick the right architecture (LLMs, RAG, ML, APIs, automation, custom logic) for the actual problem.
